How it is built

360 mmradiator, three intake fans

Tuned for airflow

Cool air enters through the front and the glass side stays clear of cables. The radiator sits in the front, so the processor and the graphics card both draw air that has not passed over the other. Fan curves are set and tested before the build leaves us.

72 hoursof stress testing per build

72-hour burn-in

Every build runs a full load test for three days before it ships. We log temperatures, clocks and fan speeds. Any part that drifts out of range is replaced and the test starts again. You get the log with your PC.

0loose cables visible through the glass

Cable-managed by hand

One technician builds each PC from start to finish. Cables run behind the motherboard tray and are bound with reusable straps. The result looks tidy and keeps the airflow path open. Our technician signs the build sheet.

Questions

How long until my PC ships?

Builds ship in days. The 72-hour burn-in is part of that time. The estimated date is shown next to the price before you add to cart.

What does the warranty cover?

Every build includes years of parts and labor, plus lifetime phone and chat support. You can extend to years when you configure your PC or in the cart.

Can I return it?

Yes. You have 30 days from delivery to return the PC for a refund. Keep the original packaging if you can, so the return travels safely.

How does 0% APR financing work?

The total is split into equal monthly payments with no interest. The monthly figure on this page is the total divided by , rounded up to the next dollar. Financing is subject to approval.

Can I upgrade parts later?

Yes. Every part is a standard retail component, and the side panel opens with two thumbscrews. Upgrades made with compatible parts do not affect your warranty.

How is the glass panel protected in transit?

The tempered glass is fitted with foam inserts inside the case, and the case ships in a double box. A reinforced crate is available if you want extra protection.
